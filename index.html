CartaCanta

English

Scorekeeper and card table for traditional Italian card games: Briscola, Scopa, Scopone scientifico, Asso piglia tutto, Tressette, Scala 40, Ramino, Burraco and Canasta. Keep score of live matches, or actually play — against bots, on the same network, or online via Game Center.
