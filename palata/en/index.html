Palata

Palata turns the Merach R15 Pro rower into a guided machine: it connects over Bluetooth, shows pace, watts, strokes per minute and heart rate, sets the level by itself during workouts and saves every session to Health. With Apple Watch you can start and follow the session from your wrist.

Frequently asked questions

Which rowers work?
Palata is made for the Merach R15 Pro, which uses the standard Bluetooth FTMS protocol. Other FTMS rowers can connect, but the resistance levels are tuned for that model.

My iPhone can't find the rower.
Wake the rower with a few strokes so the display turns on, and make sure no other app (such as the maker's own) is already connected: the rower accepts one connection at a time. Then tap Pair the rower in the app.

Can I start from Apple Watch with the app closed on iPhone?
Yes. Pick Free row or a workout on the Watch: the iPhone opens Palata in the background, connects to the rower and starts the session. Keep the iPhone near the rower.

Where are my sessions?
In the app's history, with charts, splits and CSV export, and in the Health app as rowing workouts. Deleting a session from the history does not remove it from Health.

Music doesn't start.
Palata controls Apple's Music app and needs access to your music library (Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music → Palata).
