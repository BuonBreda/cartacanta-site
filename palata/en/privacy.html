Palata — Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Data controller: Andrea Breda — donbreda4@gmail.com

In short

Palata collects no data. There is no account and no app server on the Internet, and the developer sees nothing of your workouts. No ads, trackers or analytics.

Where your data stays

Sessions (duration, distance, pace, watts, strokes, level and heart rate, second by second) and the workouts you create are stored only on your iPhone. You export them yourself, as CSV, when you choose to share them.

Health

With your permission Palata saves each session to the Health app as a rowing workout, with distance and energy, and reads your heart rate to show it during the session. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can change the permissions in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Palata.

Apple Watch

When you use Apple Watch, iPhone and Watch exchange session data directly, through Apple's services between your own devices.

Permissions

Bluetooth connects to the rower, reads its data and sets its level. Music library lets you choose and control music in the Music app while you row.

What goes through Apple

Apple may give the developer aggregate, anonymous statistics about installs and crashes, which contain nothing about you or your workouts.

Legal basis, retention and rights

Processing is limited to your devices and is needed to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); health data goes to Health only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)). Data stays on your iPhone while you use the app and is removed when you delete it; data in Health is managed from the Health app. As we hold no data on our servers, there is nothing for us to provide or erase on request. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
