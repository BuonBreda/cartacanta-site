Canasta: rules and how to play

Played with two 52-card decks plus 4 jokers (108 cards): with 2 players (15 cards each), with 3 (13), or with 4 in pairs or free-for-all (11).

Only groups of cards of equal value are melded (no runs). Jokers and 2s are wild cards: every combination must have more natural cards than wild ones.

A canasta is a group of 7 cards: pure 500 points (no wild cards), mixed 300.

Red threes are shown immediately and replaced: they are worth 100 each, 800 if one side has all four. Black threes block the discard pile.

Opening requires a minimum that grows with your score: 50 points (up to 1495), 90 (up to 2995), 120 (beyond). Values: joker 50, 2s and aces 20, king down to 8 10, 7 down to 4 and black threes 5.

Whoever goes out (running out of cards, with at least one canasta) earns +100. Play to 3000 or 5000 points.
