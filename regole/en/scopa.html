Scopa: rules and how to play

Played with a 40-card deck: 3 cards each and 4 face up on the table.

A card captures the card of equal value, or several cards whose sum equals its value. If the card of equal value is on the table, you must capture that one and not the combination.

Whoever clears the table with a capture scores a «scopa» (sweep): 1 point (it doesn't count on the last play of the deal). Cards left on the table at the end go to whoever made the last capture.

Deal points, 1 each: cards (the majority), coins, the settebello (the 7 of coins) and the primiera.

The primiera is worked out by taking the best card of each suit: seven 21, six 18, ace 16, five 15, four 14, three 13, two 12, face cards 10.

Play to 11, 16 or 21 points; with 4 players in pairs, partners facing each other.
