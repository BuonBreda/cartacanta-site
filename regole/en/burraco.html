Burraco: rules and how to play

Played with two 52-card decks plus 4 jokers (108 cards): with 2 players, with 4 in pairs, or free-for-all with 3 or 4. 11 cards each and two pozzetti (extra hands) of 11.

You meld sequences of the same suit or groups of cards of equal value (at least 3 cards), with at most one wild card: a joker or a pinella (a 2, the deuce wild).

A burraco is a combination of at least 7 cards: clean 200 points (no wild card), semi-clean 150 (there is a wild card but 7 cards are natural), dirty 100.

Whoever runs out of cards takes the pozzetto (extra hand) and keeps playing. Going out requires at least one burraco and the final discard.

Hand score: going out +100, pozzetto not taken −100, plus the value of the cards melded on the table minus those left in hand. Values: joker 30, pinella 20, ace 15, king down to 8 10, 7 down to 3 5.

Play to 505, 1005 or 2005 points.
