Pinnacola: rules and how to play

Played with two 52-card decks plus 4 jokers, with the red 2s removed (104 cards): with 2 or 3 players, or with 4 in pairs or free-for-all. 13 cards each; one face-up card starts the discard pile.

The wild cards are the jokers and the pinelle (the black 2s), at most one per combination. A 2 never counts as a natural card: runs go A-3-4 … K-A.

You meld runs of at least 3 cards of the same suit and sets of three or four cards of equal value in different suits. There is no minimum point requirement to open.

The discard pile is a row of face-up discards: you may take any card together with every card discarded after it, but the chosen card must be melded right away, in a new combination or laid off on one of your own.

You go out by running out of cards with the final discard: +100, or +200 «in hand», that is melding everything in the same turn without ever having opened before. The complete clean run from ace to ace (pinnacolone) is worth 1500 points and ends the hand at once.

Hand score: value of the melded cards minus those left in hand, which count double for whoever hasn't opened. Clean runs of 7 or more cards and clean fours of a kind count double. Values: joker 25, pinella 20, ace 15, king down to 6 10, 5 down to 3 5.

Play to 1000, 1500 or 2000 points.
