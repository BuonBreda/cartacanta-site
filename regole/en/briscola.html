Briscola: rules and how to play

Played with a 40-card deck. Values: ace 11, three 10, king 4, cavallo (knight, or queen) 3, jack 2; all other cards are worth 0.

Each player is dealt 3 cards; the last card of the deck is turned face up and sets the trump suit.

The player on lead may play any card: there is no obligation to follow suit. The trick goes to the highest trump or, if no trump was played, to the highest card of the suit led.

The trick winner draws first from the stock and leads the next trick.

At the end of the hand, count the points taken in tricks: 120 in all. The hand is won by whoever scores more than 60; at 60–60 the hand is a tie and is not awarded.

With 4 players the game is played in pairs, with partners sitting opposite each other; trick points are added up per pair. The match is played best of 3 or best of 5 hands.
