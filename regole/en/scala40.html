Scala 40: rules and how to play

Played with two 52-card decks plus 4 jokers (108 cards); 13 cards each.

To «open» you must meld combinations worth at least 40 points: sets of three or four of equal value and runs of the same suit. The ace is worth 11 (or 1 in the ace-2-3 run), face cards 10, and the joker is worth the card it replaces.

After opening you may lay off cards on your own and other players' combinations, and take back a joker by replacing it with the actual card.

You go out by running out of cards, with a final discard. In many variants going out «in hand» (without ever having melded) counts double for the opponents.

At the end of the hand whoever went out scores 0; the others add up the cards left in hand (joker 25, ace 11, face cards 10).

Whoever reaches the limit (101, 151 or 201) is eliminated. The last player left in the game wins.
