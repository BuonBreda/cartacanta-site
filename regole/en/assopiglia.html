Asso piglia tutto: rules and how to play

It is a variant of Scopa: all the classic capture and scoring rules apply.

The difference: whoever plays an ace takes all the cards on the table, whatever they are.

In many variants a capture made with the ace never counts as a scopa (sweep), even if it clears the table; and if an ace is already on the table, the ace played captures only that one (a rule to agree on beforehand).

Whoever clears the table with a normal capture scores a scopa (sweep): 1 point (not on the last play).

Deal points, 1 each: cards, coins, settebello and primiera (seven 21, six 18, ace 16, five 15…), plus the scopa points (sweeps).

Play to 11, 16 or 21 points; with 4 players in pairs, partners facing each other.
