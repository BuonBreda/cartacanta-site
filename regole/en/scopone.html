Scopone: rules and how to play

It is Scopa played by 4, in fixed pairs with partners sitting opposite each other, dealing out the whole deck in a single deal.

In the classic game each player gets 9 cards with 4 on the table; in Scopone scientifico, 10 cards each and an empty table.

The capture rules are those of Scopa: a card captures the equal card or a combination of equal sum; with the equal card on the table, the single capture is mandatory.

Whoever clears the table scores a scopa (sweep) (1 point, never on the last play). The final cards go to the last pair that made a capture.

Deal points: cards, coins, settebello and primiera (seven 21, six 18, ace 16, five 15…), 1 point each, plus the scopa points (sweeps).

Play to 11, 16 or 21; the scientifico traditionally to 21.
