Ramino: rules and how to play

Played with two 52-card decks plus jokers; 13 cards each (10 in some variants).

On your turn you draw from the stock or the discard pile, meld any combinations, and discard one card.

Combinations are sets of three/four of equal value and runs of the same suit; the joker replaces any card. Unlike Scala 40, there is usually no minimum point requirement to open (it depends on house variants).

You go out by running out of cards after the final discard; going out «in hand», melding everything in one go, counts double for the opponents in many variants.

Whoever goes out scores 0; the others add up the cards left in hand: joker 25, ace 11 (or 1), face cards 10, the others their face value.

Whoever reaches the limit (101, 151 or 201) is eliminated; the last one left wins.
