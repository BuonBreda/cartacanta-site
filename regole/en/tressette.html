Tressette: rules and how to play

Played with a 40-card deck. Trick-taking order, from strongest: 3, 2, ace, king, cavallo (knight), jack, then 7-6-5-4. There are no trumps.

You must follow the suit led; if you can't, you discard any card (which cannot win the trick). The trick goes to the highest card of the suit led.

Points: each ace is worth 1 full point; 2s, 3s and face cards are worth ⅓ of a point; the other cards 0. Whoever takes the last trick earns 1 point. Thirds are rounded down: 11 points per deal in all.

Declarations (accusi), to be made before playing your first card: buongioco (three aces, three 2s or three 3s) 3 points, napoletana (ace, 2 and 3 of the same suit) 3 points; with four equal cards, 4 points.

Play to 21, 31 or 41 points; with 4 players in pairs, partners facing each other.
