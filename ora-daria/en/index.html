Ora d'aria

The roster app for shift workers: mornings, afternoons, nights, on-call duty, post-night rest, leave and rest days, drawn on the 24 hours of the day. Photograph the ward's printed roster and the app reads it entirely on device, or copy it in with the sequential keypad. One tap exports the month to the system calendar, and the widget keeps today and tomorrow on your Home Screen.

Your data lives in your iCloud: no account to create, no server of ours, no ads.
